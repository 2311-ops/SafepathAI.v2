## Accomplishments
- "Remove from circle" `InkWell` now has `borderRadius: BorderRadius.circular(8)` and wraps its `Row` in `Padding(vertical: 12, horizontal: 8)`, reaching a >=44 logical-pixel tappable area without touching the visible icon size, text size, or color.
- "Remove from circle" label now uses `AppTypography.bodySecondary.copyWith(color: AppColors.sosRedDeep, fontWeight: FontWeight.w600)` instead of a standalone hardcoded `TextStyle(fontSize: 13, ...)`.
- The `familyId == null` empty state now renders `Icons.group_off` above an `AppTypography.heading` "No circle yet" title and a new `AppTypography.bodySecondary` explanatory line, centered with `AppSpacing.lg` padding — matching `privacy_center_screen.dart`'s `_PrivacyMessage` composition.
- The `otherMembers.isEmpty` empty state now renders `Icons.diversity_3` above the existing "Just you so far" title, plus a new `AppTypography.bodySecondary` invite line — matching `invite_member_screen.dart`'s "Create a circle first" card composition, without adopting its `SafePathCard` chrome.
