## Phase Goal

**As a** family member in danger, **I want to** trigger an SOS that instantly reaches my guardians and emergency contacts with my live location, **so that** help can find me within seconds even if I have no signal, my phone is locked, or the app is closed.

<objective>
Turn a static alert into a moving one. After an SOS the sender's position streams to their responders for a fixed, server-decided window, the responder sees exactly how long is left, and the stream survives the sender locking their phone.

Purpose: SOS-04 is the requirement and D-21 fixes its shape — a fixed window with an explicit end time and countdown. D-04 requires the sender's own emergency session to show live-location sharing status alongside the delivery state plan 03-04 already renders, and this plan supplies that half. D-31 is the decision that makes it real: the sender in an emergency will not reliably keep the app in the foreground, so the feed must survive backgrounding via `flutter_foreground_task` scoped specifically to the active SOS window rather than to routine tracking.

This resolves `03-RESEARCH.md`'s Open Question 2 in favour of background survival, per D-31.

Output: server-authoritative window boundaries, the window-gated location ingest command, the `LiveLocationWindowUpdate` broadcast, the SOS-scoped foreground-task location service, the non-jittering countdown, and the live-active rendering on both the sender's and the responder's screens.

Explicit boundaries: this location stream is entirely separate from Phase 2's routine `ReportLocationCommand` pipeline — no SOS position ever enters the routine batching or sharing-preference path, and no routine ping enters this one. The foreground service starts only when an SOS session goes live and stops the moment the window closes or the session is canceled; it is not a general background-tracking capability.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| sender device → `POST /sos/{id}/location` | A client asserts a position and a timestamp for an emergency session. |
| backend → `/hubs/alert` recipients | Precise continuous location of one family member is fanned out to others. |
| OS → foreground service | A background location capability is activated on the sender's device. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
